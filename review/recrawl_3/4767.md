### Kinlochleven Community Benefit Fund Grants
- **description**: The charity is set-up to give grants to individuals and organisations that provide community benefit to Kinlochleven and its residents. The charity distributes funds three or four times per year and the trustees meet to decide on applications using a scoring matrix and award grants.
- **eligibility_criteria**: Applicants must provide community benefit to Kinlochleven and its residents within the area represented by the Kinlochleven Community Council. Purposes include advancement of community development and rural/urban regeneration, education, health, citizenship, arts/heritage/culture/science/technology, public participation in sport, recreational facilities/activities, environmental protection/improvement, and relief of those in need by reason of age, ill-health, disability, diversity and other disadvantage.
- **beneficiary_groups**: No specific group, or for the benefit of the community; residents of Kinlochleven and the area represented by the Kinlochleven Community Council.
