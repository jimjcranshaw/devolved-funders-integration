### North Shore Charitable Trust Small Grants
- **description**: The charity was established with a gift to create an expendable endowment fund, so that the annual income can be used to award small grants to local community-based groups which further the charitable purposes of the organisation.
- **eligibility_criteria**: Local community-based groups which further the charitable purposes of the organisation. The Trustees do not have the capacity to respond to unsolicited applications from large regional and national charities.
- **beneficiary_groups**: No specific group, or for the benefit of the community; Other charities or voluntary bodies
