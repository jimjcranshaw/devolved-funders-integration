### The Sam Crawford Charitable Trust Grant Funding
- **description**: The Trustees shall hold the Trust Fund for charitable purposes exclusively, which benefit the public in the UK and elsewhere. The Trust provides grants or donations to benefit young people, primarily through supporting organisations that prevent or provide relief from poverty, advance education, provide recreational facilities or activities, and advance public participation in sport for disadvantaged children and young adults.
- **eligibility_criteria**: The charity provides grants or donations to benefit young people. Priority is given to donating to facilities or activities benefitting disadvantaged children or young adults. Financial assistance is provided to organisations, including bodies recognised as charities in Scotland, England or Wales.
- **beneficiary_groups**: Children or young people; Other charities or voluntary bodies
