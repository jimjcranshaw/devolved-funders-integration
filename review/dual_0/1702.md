### Friends of Highland Music Grants
- **description**: FoHM's Grant Scheme was set up to assist musicians, students and groups in the study, performance and promotion of Scottish Traditional Music, Song and Dance. The scheme is currently suspended pending review.
- **funding_amounts**: Recent awards include: £975 towards Music Books and a Workshop for a Youth Band; £1500 towards the costs of staging a series of local area concerts; £1250 towards the costs of a Step Dancers Group performance; £300 towards the staging of a local North Coast Concert; £1500 x 2 to assist with CD Production by two independent artists; £1400 to support a series of seven Traditional Music events in Croy; £500 to support the production of a pipe-related CD; £1500 to support CD production; £250 and £300 for piping tuition; £200 to assist a youngster attend a piping and drumming course in Skye; £300 to assist a young clarsach player attend the Edinburgh Harp Festival; £200 towards master class tuition for a young fiddler; £800 to assist a school ceilidh band's trip to Canada; £590 towards a young duo's CD production; £250 towards a young music group's trip to Orkney.
- **deadlines**: No deadlines are stated on the website. The scheme is currently suspended pending review.
