### SHRET General Funding (Projects, Courses, Research and Therapies)
- **description**: SHRET offers funding for students and practitioners to attend courses, conduct research, undertake projects and offer a variety of therapies in various settings. It also helps make homeopathy more accessible to patients by funding clinics in conjunction with the Homeopathy-UK Charity.
