### GMTT Training Funding
- **description**: Financial contribution towards a wide range of training and development provided by recognised training providers, for employers and individuals in the motor industry in the North East of Scotland.
- **funding_amounts**: Normally expect to award up to 30% of the training costs (excluding VAT) up to a maximum of £500 for each course undertaken.
- **deadlines**: Applications must be submitted in advance of any training commencing.
