### Small Grants
- **description**: There is no deadline for our small grants programme, and you can apply for up to £20,000 at any time of year. These are assessed on a rolling basis. The programme typically makes awards to support both: Running costs for small, community based initiatives, often which are volunteer led (can apply for up to £20k in total but no more than £10k per year) and One-off pieces of work which can help develop a charity, such as the purchase of equipment/infrastructure; the development of partnership work or service evaluation (up to £20k no more than one year).
- **funding_amounts**: Up to £20,000 (can apply for up to £20k in total but no more than £10k per year for running costs; up to £20k no more than one year for one-off pieces of work).
- **deadlines**: There is no deadline for our small grants programme. Applications are assessed on a rolling basis.

### Main Grants
- **description**: We award our main grants twice a year. These are grants of more than £20,000, and are typically multi-year. For our next round, Spring 2027, our Trustees will be looking to make 3-year grants of up to £55,000. This means that applicants can split the total request of up to £55,000 over 3 years, e.g. £18,250 per year, if this level of funding is required. We are still very happy to assess grant requests up to this figure, if that is a better fit with your costs/funding profile.
- **funding_amounts**: More than £20,000, typically multi-year. For Spring 2027, Trustees will be looking to make 3-year grants of up to £55,000.
- **deadlines**: Tackling Child Abuse & Neglect – 9th Mar 2027 11:00:am; Medical Research – 9th Mar 2027 11:00:am; Animal Welfare – 9th Mar 2027 11:00:am; Neurological Conditions – 9th Mar 2027 11:00:am. Visual Impairment – Autumn 2027 (date tbc).
