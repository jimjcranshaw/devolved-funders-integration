### Community Heritage Grants
- **description**: Launched in May 2021, the Community Heritage Grant Fund supports community projects that conserve or enhance the historic environment within Perth and Kinross. The Trust recognises how difficult it can be for community groups to access funding for heritage projects and has committed over £70,000 towards local heritage initiatives to date.
- **funding_amounts**: The Trust considers applications for grants of up to £5,000, although larger sums may be considered in exceptional circumstances. A minimum award has not been set.
- **deadlines**: There is no deadline to apply for the Community Heritage Grants Scheme. The Trust welcome and encourage applications at any time.
