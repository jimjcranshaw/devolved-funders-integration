### Macaulay Development Trust Grant Programme
- **description**: The Trust's grant programme supports excellent research into the sustainable use of land and natural resources, for the benefit of people, their communities and the environment, aligning with the vision and legacy of TB Macaulay. The Trust is likely to give priority to issues which relate to Scotland, Scotland's issues with international relevance, and international issues with relevance to Scotland. The programme is currently suspended.
- **deadlines**: Applications are considered throughout the year. At present the Macaulay Development Trust's grant programme is suspended.
