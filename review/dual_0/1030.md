### Glasgow Educational and Marshall Trust General Grant
- **description**: A grant-giving trust for the people of Glasgow, providing financial support to individuals and schools for educational purposes. On average, the Trust supports 60 applications per year up to a total value of £60,000.
- **funding_amounts**: The maximum amount available per application is £1,650, but in normal circumstances it is likely that applicants will receive a lower award. Awards usually range from £200 to £1,200. For school trips, the maximum grant available is £1,500.
- **deadlines**: Applications for courses starting after 1 January 2027 must be submitted by 8th November 2026. Applications for courses starting in or before September are no longer being considered.

### School Trips Grant
- **description**: Financial support is available for Glasgow schools to allocate towards transport costs for school trips.
- **funding_amounts**: The maximum grant available is £1,500.
