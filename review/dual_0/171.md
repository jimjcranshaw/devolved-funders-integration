### Rotary Club of Langholm Trust Fund — General Charitable Grants
- **description**: The Trust Fund holds the Club's charitable money and pays it out to people in need around Langholm, to the 40-odd local organisations that walk under its umbrella each year, and to causes further afield. The major activity is organising the community umbrella walk where approximately 40 local organisations take part raising funds for their charitable and community work.
