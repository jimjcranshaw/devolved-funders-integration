### Grants to Individuals
- **description**: Grants to individuals who are members or intending members of the British Merchant Navy, particularly those experiencing financial hardship or difficulty. In the last financial year the Trust supported sixteen individuals who studied for a career in the merchant navy.
- **funding_amounts**: Grants to individuals tend to range from a few hundred to a thousand pounds.
- **deadlines**: Individuals wishing to apply for a grant can do so at any stage. Decisions can be expected within one to two months.

### Charitable Donations to Organisations
- **description**: Charitable donations to charities that offer assistance to retired members of the British Merchant Navy. In the last financial year the Trust supported three charities that offer assistance to retired members of the British Merchant Navy.
- **funding_amounts**: Charitable donations tend to exceed the range for individuals (a few hundred to a thousand pounds). In the last financial year the Trust gave just under £30,000 in total.
- **deadlines**: Charitable donations are made once a year in the Autumn.
