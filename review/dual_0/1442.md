### Gordon Cook Foundation Grants (Proactive Commissioning)
- **description**: The Foundation operates a proactive policy in making grants to other bodies. This proactive policy means that the Foundation itself identifies areas of work in Values Education which would benefit from further work and development, and commissions a preferred manager to undertake the work. The Foundation does not normally invite or respond to unsolicited applications for grant aid.
- **deadlines**: Not specified on the website. The Foundation operates a proactive policy and commissions work on an ongoing basis.
