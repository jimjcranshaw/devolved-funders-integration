### Camphill Foundation Grants and Loans
- **description**: The Foundation primarily supports the work of Camphill communities and new initiatives in the UK and the Republic of Ireland. It does this by offering small grants and low interest loans which are often needed to kickstart a project. It also makes contributions towards worthy projects outside the UK and Ireland in limited circumstances.
- **funding_amounts**: Grants are currently limited to a maximum value of £30,000 per application. Low-interest loans can be applied for, up to the value of £100,000. (Figures are in the region of)
- **deadlines**: Applications will be considered at one of our two meetings, which are held in Spring and Autumn.
