### Grants to Postgraduate Students
- **description**: Grants of around £500-£1,500 each year for postgraduate study, awarded on the basis of need. The Trust invites applications and the Trustees meet to determine those most in need. The Trust also has partnerships with some universities who specifically identify care experienced students who are in need of assistance with their postgraduate studies.
- **funding_amounts**: Around £500-£1,500 per grant. Total annual awards of around £150,000 across the Trust.
- **deadlines**: Monday 31 May 2027. This is for eligible students who will be studying during academic session September 2027 – June 2028. Applications are currently closed and will reopen from early January 2027 onwards.

### Grants to Charitable Arts Organisations
- **description**: The Trust supports arts organisations that remove socioeconomic barriers to participation in culture or educational activities. The Trust invites applications from organisations who wish to undertake projects to help those who are disadvantaged to enable them to progress and develop their potential in various ways. There are two submission deadlines annually, usually towards the end of April and the end of October.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified on the website. Total annual awards of around £150,000 across the Trust.
- **deadlines**: Friday 30 October 2026, 5pm. Friday 30 April 2027, 5pm. Two submission deadlines annually, usually towards the end of April and the end of October.
