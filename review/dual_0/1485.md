### Stafford Trust General Grants
- **description**: The Trust will consider applications for grants from charities registered in the United Kingdom, with preference being given to charitable organisations operating in Scotland. The Trust makes grants to charities from the income generated from the trust fund.
- **funding_amounts**: Grants vary, but most lie between £500 and £5,000.
- **deadlines**: The window for new applications for the Spring 2027 meeting will close on 31st December 2026. The Trustees usually meet twice per annum, in Spring and Autumn.
