### Leith Industrial School Trust General Grants
- **description**: The Trust makes small grants to organisations that work with young people in Leith. Grants are for specific projects or one-off activities that provide educational, mental, physical, moral or social benefit to children and young people ordinarily resident in the Leith area.
- **funding_amounts**: The Trust does not generally operate a maximum/minimum grant rule, but in the past our grants have generally been less than £500 per application. In exceptional circumstances we may consider awards of up to £2,000. The governors may make a partial award.
- **deadlines**: The funding deadline is on 30 September each year for a decision in November of that year.
