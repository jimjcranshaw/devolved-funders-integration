### Pleasance Trust Grants
- **description**: The Trust makes grants to voluntary organisations in the Edinburgh area which are concerned with the welfare of young people, in particular, through the encouragement of participation in outdoor activities. In view of the scale of the Trust, grants are made mainly to small voluntary organisations.
- **funding_amounts**: In the course of a year the Trust distributes about £20,000 in individual grants ranging from about £500 to about £1,500.
- **deadlines**: The trustees meet approximately quarterly, when they consider all applications received since the previous meeting. No specific deadlines are listed.
