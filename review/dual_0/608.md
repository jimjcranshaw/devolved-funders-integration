### Stirlingshire Educational Trust General Grants
- **description**: The Trust exists to give grants for educational purposes to either individuals or organisations from the area of the County of Stirling. Grants are awarded across a range of categories including post-graduate scholarships, supplementary bursaries, special grants for over 21s, the John Allan Bursary, travel scholarships and grants, educational excursions, educational travel, special equipment for schools, support of clubs, adult education, visual arts, music, drama, and educational experiments and research.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified on the website. Awards may be made towards fees, books, travel, equipment, kit etc. It should be noted that SET have limited funds from which to make awards.
- **deadlines**: The Governors meet on the 1st Wednesday of June, September, December and March - please ensure your application is made at least 3 weeks before the date of the meeting.
