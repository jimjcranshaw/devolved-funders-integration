### Novum Trust Small Short-Term Grants
- **description**: The Novum Trust provides small short-term grants to initiate projects in Christian action and research which cannot readily be financed from other sources. 'Novum' literally means 'a new thing'. The Trustees are guided to support new work, aiming to 'prime the pump' and allow a new enterprise the chance to prove itself. All awards should be considered as one-offs.
- **funding_amounts**: For general guidance, grants are typically between £500 and £2,500.
