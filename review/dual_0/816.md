### Royal Celtic Society Grants
- **description**: The Society gives grants to support the language, literature, music and culture of the Highlands and Western Islands of Scotland. Grants are awarded for original composition in Gaelic prose or verse, for skill in the composition or execution of music, for proficiency in the arts, music and industries of Scotland, and for such other merit as the Society may think desirable to recognise.
