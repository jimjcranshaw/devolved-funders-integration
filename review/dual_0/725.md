### Alexander Moncur Trust General Grants
- **description**: The Alexander Moncur Trust awards multiple grants, generally up to £5,000, twice a year. In exceptional circumstances, the Trust will award grants significantly in excess of £5,000. The Trust's primary aim is to support cultural, educational, health and social projects within Dundee and its environs.
- **funding_amounts**: Generally up to £5,000. In exceptional circumstances, grants significantly in excess of £5,000. In the past, multi-year arrangements of £10,000 per year for three years have been awarded.
- **deadlines**: Deadline for applications for the Spring meeting is 31st March. Deadline for applications for the Autumn meeting is 30th September.
