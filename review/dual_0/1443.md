### The Courant Fund for Children Grant
- **description**: The Courant Fund provides grants to assist children and young people up to the age of 18 across the Lothians, who face poverty, ill-health or other difficulties. Applications come from frontline professionals who have direct contact with the child or children concerned.
- **funding_amounts**: Grants are typically less than £500. You may request more, but please consider whether a smaller amount would meet the need. Most Courant Fund grants are under £500. You can still submit, but trustees give priority to requests within this typical range.
- **deadlines**: There's no submission deadline: trustees normally meet twice a year, in May and November (the exact date varies), and consider every application received since their last meeting.
