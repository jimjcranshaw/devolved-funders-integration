### Piobaireachd Society Grant Funding & Bursaries
- **description**: The Society has a long history of support for piping, and in particular ceòl mòr. Our Grant Application Form has been formulated to facilitate and formalise applications for financial assistance from the Society. Applications from both organisations and individuals are accepted.
- **funding_amounts**: Individuals, Small Events: up to £1,000; Medium Events: up to £1,500; Large Events: £1,500+; Prize donations books etc. (Juvenile/Amateur Contests).
- **deadlines**: There are two deadlines for applications: March 15, and October 15.
