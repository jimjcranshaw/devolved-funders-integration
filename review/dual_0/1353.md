### The Adamson Trust Holiday/Respite Break Grant
- **description**: The Adamson Trust provides grants towards funding holidays, or respite breaks, for children aged 2-17 years of age who have continuing physical health problems or learning disabilities. Applications may be made by individual families or by organisations such as schools, social work departments or hospital services.
- **funding_amounts**: The average payment dispensed is currently £250 per application. A maximum of three awards may be provided to individual applicants, including multiple family applicants.
- **deadlines**: Meeting Date / Application Due Date: February / 31 December; May / 31 March; August / 30 June; November / 30 September.
