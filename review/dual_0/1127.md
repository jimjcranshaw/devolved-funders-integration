### Arbroath Guildry Incorporation Grants
- **description**: The Guildry makes grants to voluntary organisations, charities and individuals. It also supports local schools and colleges. Grants are made from available revenue but not capital. The Dean and Council normally meet in April and October to consider grants and the full Guildry meets in May and November to ratify or approve grants given.
- **deadlines**: The Dean and Council normally meet in April and October to consider grants. The full Guildry meets in May and November to ratify or approve grants given. No specific application deadlines are stated.
