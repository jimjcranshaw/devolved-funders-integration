### Mugdock Children's Trust Grant
- **description**: The Trust provides grants, typically of £500 - £1000, to registered charities whose object is the support and benefit of children in need, most often in the West of Scotland area. This may be due to disability, illness, either physical or psychological, or because of deprivation of any kind. The Trust deed requires that grants are used within Scotland.
- **funding_amounts**: Typically, grants of £500 - £1000 per successful application are given, unless in exceptional circumstances.
- **deadlines**: Applications should be submitted by 1st February and 1st September each year for consideration by the Trustees who meet biannually in Spring and Autumn.
