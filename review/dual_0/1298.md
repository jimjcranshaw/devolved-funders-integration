### Angus Educational Trust Grant
- **description**: The Angus Educational Trust is responsible for administering grants to eligible children and young people in support of their education. All applications, with the exception of the Morgan Emergency Grant, are considered at scheduled meetings by the Board of Governors. The Board meets twice per school year, in September and March.
- **funding_amounts**: No specific grant amounts are stated on the available pages. Annual expenditure figures: £25,422 (2022), £21,180 (2023), £27,893 (2024).
- **deadlines**: Applications are considered at Board meetings held twice per school year, in September and March. The Morgan Emergency Grant is an exception to this schedule.
