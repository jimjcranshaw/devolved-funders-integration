### Rotary Club of Kirkcaldy Charitable Trust General Fund
- **description**: The Trust holds and applies investments and monies received from the Club, donations, bequests, Deeds of Covenant or any other source, to charitable objects and purposes. Funds are applied to or for the relief of the poor and needy or to or for such other charitable purpose, institution, society or object as the Club shall in duly constituted meeting from time to time direct. The charity aims to spend most of its funds locally, although there are some national and international activities which they support.
