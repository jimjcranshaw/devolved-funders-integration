### The Maple Trust General Grants Programme
- **description**: The Maple Trust provides financial support to charities registered in the United Kingdom, with preference given to organisations with projects based in Scotland. Following a review in 2025, the focus of the Trust's grant making is now on Children and Young People – Scotland-wide. Applications are considered by the trustees at their bi-annual meetings (February and August).
- **funding_amounts**: We will accept funding requests up to £10k.
- **deadlines**: Applications will be considered by the trustees at their bi-annual meetings (Feb and Aug).
