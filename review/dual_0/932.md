### Leith Benevolent Association Grants
- **description**: The Leith Benevolent Association exists to provide funding for projects or activities run by individuals or groups for the benefit of people in or linked to Leith. The Association has already supported a wide range of projects with funding from £50 to £2,500. Activities supported include a befriending service for dementia sufferers, support for older people's groups and youth clubs.
- **funding_amounts**: Funding from £50 to £2,500. Applications should be thought of in terms of a few hundred or thousands of pounds per annum, rather than tens of thousands.
- **deadlines**: All applications should be received by the Administrator no later than by the end of August and January preceding each meeting. The Trustees generally determine awards at their meetings in September and February.
