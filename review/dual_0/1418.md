### Bell's Nautical Trust Annual Grant
- **description**: The Trust provides grants to enhance maritime education in Scotland. Organisations engaged in maritime pursuits, especially recognised training, are welcome to apply for assistance. Grants tend to range from a couple of hundred to a few thousand pounds. In a typical year 10 – 15 organisations are supported with a combined total of around £15,000.
