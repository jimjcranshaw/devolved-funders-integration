### Rotary Club of Broughty Ferry Trust Fund
- **description**: The charitable arm of the Rotary Club of Broughty Ferry (RCBF). The Trust Fund provides financial support to charitable purposes, institutions, societies or objects as directed by the Club. In directing financial support from the Trust Fund, this is offered in the first instance, but not exclusively, to locally-based charities with little by way of reserves, regularly working in conjunction with the Club.
