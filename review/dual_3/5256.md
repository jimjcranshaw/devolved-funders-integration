### The Pat Cullinan Charitable Trust Grant
- **description**: The Trust provides grants to individuals and other registered charities or activities with clearly defined charitable purposes. The Trust's charitable purposes are the relief of poverty and poor health, the promotion of education, and the promotion of religion. The Trust will consider exceptional proposals which fit closely their specific areas of interest.
- **deadlines**: Not specified on the website. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis with a 4-week response time for successful candidates.
