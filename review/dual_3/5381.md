### The Local History Fund
- **description**: A capital fund of £150,000 from which the Trust draws interest to support applications for funding. The fund aids local historical studies in the nine counties of historic Ulster through financial aid and by advice and encouragement.
- **funding_amounts**: Help is normally provided through small grants and awards of more than £1,000 are now exceptional.
- **deadlines**: The closing date for the next round of applications is 28 February 2027. The closing date for the current round of grants is 28th February 2027. For indexing grants, the closing date for the current round of grants is 31 August 2026.
