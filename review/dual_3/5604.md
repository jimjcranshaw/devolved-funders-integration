### Civic Dollars
- **description**: Civic Dollars is a project that rewards people for volunteering and participating in healthy activities. Participants earn 'Civic Dollars' which can be donated to local charities or community groups. DHC acts as a broker, distributing funds to the nominated organisations.
- **funding_amounts**: Variable, depending on the number of Civic Dollars donated.
- **deadlines**: Ongoing.
