### Deaf Scots Trust Grant
- **description**: A single annual grant pot providing support, provision and grants to deaf and deafblind people and groups in Scotland, in order to advance their education, health, well-being, mental health, arts, heritage and culture. Trustees award funding once a year to individuals, community groups and small charities.
- **funding_amounts**: We will consider all applications for up to £1,000. We would particularly welcome applications looking for less than £1,000 so we can reach out to more people with Deaf Scots Trust’s money. Historic awards: £3,200 shared with 3 recipients (2025/2026); £3,787 shared with 4 groups/organisations (2024/2025); £4,464 shared with 5 recipients (2023/2024); £3,342 shared with 5 recipients (2022/2023); £3,800 shared with 5 recipients (2021/2022).
- **deadlines**: We aim to support projects, groups or organisations and Trustees will make a decision in Autumn 2027. Trustees have made a decision due to the limited funds we have to only grant funding on an annual basis i.e. once a year. Trustees released funding to 4 groups in September 2025.
