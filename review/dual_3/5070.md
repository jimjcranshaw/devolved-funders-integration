### Schools Education Fund
- **description**: The Education Fund was launched in January 2026 for schools within the Foundation's Area of Benefit. It awarded £112,370 to schools for trips, outdoor play areas and food and nutrition projects. The fund was open for applications from 12/01/2026 until 20/03/2026 and is now closed.

### Inspiring Change Fund
- **description**: The Inspiring Change Fund is the Foundation's programme for larger grants.
