## Extracted opportunities (3)
### 250th Anniversary Focus Charity Project
- **description**: The Society is partnering as a donor to support the redevelopment of the West Highland Museum in Fort William. The goal is to contribute to the redevelopment and create a specific room or space where the Highland Society can display artefacts, support visiting art collections, and help educate local children and residents about the history, traditions, and culture of the Lochaber region.
- **funding_amounts**: The Society aims to raise at least £100k towards this project. An initial £20,000 was raised and transferred as a grant.
- **deadlines**: The project may be approved to start in late 2026 and could be complete on or around the 250th anniversary in 2028.

### Highland Society of London Prize for Best Fiction Book (Gaelic Literature Awards)
- **description**: The Society sponsors a prize for the Best Fiction Book as part of the Gaelic Literature Awards, administered by the Gaelic Books Council.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified.
- **deadlines**: Books published between 1 May 2025 and 30 April 2026. Winners announced on 15th September 2026.

### Dissertation Prize, University of the Highlands and Islands
- **description**: The Society awards a prize for a final-year dissertation on a Highland theme by a student from the University of the Highlands and Islands.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified.
- **deadlines**: Announced 10th September 2026.
