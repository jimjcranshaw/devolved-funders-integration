### Rescue and Rehoming Grants
- **description**: We provide one-off grants to UK-based pet rescue organisations to support their essential work. Funding can be used for day-to-day costs, equipment and improvements to facilities.
- **funding_amounts**: As a guide, we’re unlikely to award more than £100,000 as a one-off grant. If you’re considering a request over £50,000, please contact us before applying.
- **deadlines**: Applications for less than £50,000 are reviewed monthly. Due to the volume of applications we receive, decisions can take up to 4–5 months. If we need more information, this may extend the timeframe.

### Preventing Pet Relinquishment Grants
- **description**: This programme supports projects that help keep pets in loving homes and out of rescue. Funding is available for both one-off and multi-year initiatives that support pet owners in times of crisis or help reduce stray populations.
- **funding_amounts**: As a guide, we’re unlikely to award more than £100,000 as a one-off grant.
- **deadlines**: Applications are reviewed three times a year. Due to the volume of applications we receive, decisions can take up to 4–5 months. If we need more information, this may extend the timeframe.
