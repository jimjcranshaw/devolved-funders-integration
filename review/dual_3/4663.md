### General Fund Grants for Individuals and Families
- **description**: The General fund is for grants to individuals and families in need. The aim of the Fund is the prevention or relief for the purposes of social welfare of children and young people of poverty and/or to assist those in need experiencing illness or disability. A proportion of the funds are allocated in Edinburgh. The Trust also administers grants on behalf of the Ponton Trust which supports vulnerable and disadvantaged children and young people in Edinburgh and the Lothians.
- **deadlines**: From Wednesday 1st April 2026 the application form is available to referral partners.

### Grants for small Charities
- **description**: The Edinburgh & Lothian Trust Fund SCIO supports small charities who prevent or relieve poverty and/or assist those in need experiencing illness or disability. The Trust’s funds are restricted to beneficiaries who reside in the City of Edinburgh or the Lothians.
- **funding_amounts**: The Trustees will consider applications for up to £2,000.
- **deadlines**: CLOSED UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE. Grant dates and deadline applications for 2024-25 are as follows: Trustees meeting Tuesday 7 May 2024, Deadline for applications Tuesday 16 April 2024; Trustees meeting Tuesday 6 August 2024, Deadline for applications Tuesday 16 July 2024; Trustees meeting Tuesday 5 November 2024, closed until further notice; Trustees meeting Tuesday 4 February 2025, closed until further notice.

### Edinburgh Police Fund for Children
- **description**: The Fund is for the provision of school shoes and warm coats to children of school age. Outerwear bearing the school logo is not eligible.

### The Edinburgh Fire Fund
- **description**: Assistance for Residents of the City of Edinburgh affected by Fire. The purpose of the Edinburgh Fire Fund, is to relieve the suffering caused through injury or financial loss as a result of a fire.
- **deadlines**: Applications will only be accepted for fires that have occurred within the last six months.
