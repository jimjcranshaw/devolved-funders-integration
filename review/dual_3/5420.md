### Belfast Masonic Charity Fund (B.M.C.F.) General Assistance
- **description**: The Fund was founded in 1862 to provide relief to poor and distressed Freemasons and/or their families. It offers financial assistance and support to eligible individuals within its catchment area.
- **funding_amounts**: No specific funding amounts are stated on the website.
- **deadlines**: General Committee meetings are held on the last Friday of January, February, March, April, May, September, October, and November of each year. An Emergency Committee is available as necessary outside these dates, including during June, July, August, and December.
