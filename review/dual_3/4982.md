### Grants for Individuals
- **description**: Grants to support individual young people aged 16-30 who are actively engaged in Music or the Visual Arts and who are in genuine need of financial support to pursue their artistic goals. Priority is given to providing grants to individuals.
- **funding_amounts**: The minimum grant size we offer is £300 and the maximum grant is £3,000. Grants are normally for one year but in exceptional circumstances, for instance if the sustainability of the project could be at risk, the trustees may agree to a longer period.
- **deadlines**: Applications are now closed. We will be open for new applications in February 2027. Applications will be dealt with at fixed times during the year.

### Grants for Charitable Organisations
- **description**: Grants to small charitable organisations based in Edinburgh and the Lothians that work directly with young artists who meet the Trust's eligibility criteria. The Trust will accept applications from small charitable organisations who work directly with young artists and are able to channel support directly to young people who meet our eligibility criteria.
- **funding_amounts**: The minimum grant size we offer is £300 and the maximum grant is £3,000. Grants are normally for one year but in exceptional circumstances, for instance if the sustainability of the project could be at risk, the trustees may agree to a longer period.
- **deadlines**: Applications are now closed. We will be open for new applications in February 2027. Applications will be dealt with at fixed times during the year.
