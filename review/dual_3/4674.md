### Grants for Women and Girls in Scotland
- **description**: The Women's Fund for Scotland exclusively supports community-led organisations across Scotland that exist to strengthen women and girls. Grants support work that builds confidence, leadership and participation; tackles poverty and economic inequality; prevents violence and creates safer spaces; reduces isolation and strengthens community connection; and opens pathways to education, employment and influence.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified on the website. A modest grant can mean: A new community space in the Highlands; Transport costs for rural women in the Borders; Trauma-informed training for volunteers in Dundee; A mentoring programme for teenage girls in Glasgow.
- **deadlines**: Not specified on the website. Contact Foundation Scotland for current deadlines.
