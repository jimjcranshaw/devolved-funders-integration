### The Northern Meeting Charity Grants
- **description**: The Northern Meeting Charity makes grants to charitable organisations and individuals in the seven northern counties of Scotland. Since 2016 they have made 60 grants and given out some £90,000. They have favoured grants to young, disadvantaged and disabled people within the Highlands. Grants have supported piping and drumming tuition, learning the harp and clarsach, education, sport, adventure, community development, dementia support, drama groups, riding groups, disabled ramblers, food larders and breakfasts for poor families.
