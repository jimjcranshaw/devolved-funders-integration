### Responsive Grants
- **description**: The Responsive Grant-Making programme is the main programme where the charity considers applications for support. Grants are usually unrestricted one-off grants of around £750 – £1,000 each, going into the charity's general funds to support core costs. The charity aims to make around 30 responsive grants per year with an average value of at least £750 each, although more grants or larger amounts may be awarded when sufficient funds are available.
- **funding_amounts**: Usually unrestricted one-off grants of around £750 – £1,000 each. Occasionally the trustees may award other amounts (and for a particularly strong applications we may occasionally make a larger award). We aim to make around 30 responsive grants per year with an average value of least £750 each, although more grants or larger amounts may be awarded when we have sufficient funds.
- **deadlines**: The final deadline for Responsive grant applications is 1 February 2027 (but we recommend applying sooner if possible). Our final awards will be made in March 2027.

### Planned Grants
- **description**: A proportion of our funds are committed to our Planned Grant-Making programme – through this we make contributions to national charities which the trustees have decided, as a matter of policy, to support on a long term basis. Each year we support 10-12 charities under this programme, working in different fields, with grants of at least £750 each. Grants are now awarded each year at the February trustees meeting, but the areas of work and the organisations to be considered are reviewed every few years and new organisations may be added – but only on the basis of trustees' personal recommendations.
- **funding_amounts**: Grants of at least £750 each. Regular grants (normally now £1,000/year, occasionally more).
- **deadlines**: Grants are now awarded each year at the February trustees meeting. The last round of Planned Grants will be made in February/March 2027.

### Blue Sky/Collaborative Grants
- **description**: Each year for several years The Paristamen Charity has invited charities to propose an imaginative "Blue Sky" initiative as a one-off project where we say: "If we offer you £20K (or occasionally, £25K) what could you do?" in a particular field. The theme varies from year to year. From 2021 these are described as Blue Sky grants though in the past we simply used the term Large Grants. From 2024 we introduced the term Collaborative Grants where we work with another charity to administer certain grants. Over the years, the themes of our large grants vary between different charitable aims.
- **funding_amounts**: One-off grant of £20,000 (or occasionally £25,000).
- **deadlines**: The last Blue Sky award was made in March 2026. No more applications or expressions of interest for Blue Sky Grants can therefore be considered.
