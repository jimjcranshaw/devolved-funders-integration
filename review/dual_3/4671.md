### Kirk Murdoch Charitable Trust Grant
- **description**: The aim of the trust is to provide much needed financial support to young people in Scotland, whose lives have been impacted by cancer, who participate in sport. This can mean those who perform in their chosen sport to a very high level and others for whom sport is part of their life but more of an enthusiastic pastime. As well as direct financial grants to support young people we also make donations to fellow charitable institutions whose objectives align with ours.
- **deadlines**: We accept grant applications throughout the year.
