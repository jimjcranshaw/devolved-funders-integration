### Environmental Grant Making
- **description**: Ma’at Environment Fund is a private philanthropic fund established in 2023 to engage in environmental philanthropy through charitable giving and impact investment, focusing on the marine environment, wildlife conservation, and projects that: Make the world a better place; Protect & restore the natural environment; Have a strong community involvement/impact; Bring joy to the soul; Present an opportunity to contribute not just money but time and expertise and to develop a relationship with the beneficiaries/investees. Ma’at supports projects that use technology to affect environmental change and empower local people with a strong element of community engagement. We do this with a mix of grant-making and investment.
