### Blue Carbon Conservation Project Grant
- **description**: A flexible, independent and collaborative grant-giving model which enables local solutions to generate a positive impact for the ocean, climate and communities. Funding is provided for blue carbon conservation projects, including match funding.
- **funding_amounts**: A maximum of £20,000 per grant application, but happy to consider applications which require a higher amount. Match funding of up to £20,000 is also available if you have already secured funding or are putting in a bid for funding which requires match funding.
- **deadlines**: No specific deadlines stated. Contact the Trust early when considering funding of your project.
