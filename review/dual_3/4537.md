### Lady Margaret Skiffington Trust Grant
- **description**: The trustees welcome letters of application for grants from visually impaired or blind persons living in East Fife or who come to East Fife for training and rehabilitation. The grants are intended to benefit partially sighted or blind persons so as to ameliorate their quality of life.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified. The Trust requires quotations based on the amount: up to £1,000 – one; £1,000 to £3,000 – two; over £3,000 – three.
- **deadlines**: No specific deadlines mentioned. Applications are welcome and considered on an ongoing basis.
