### Neilston Windfarm Legacy Community Grants
- **description**: Grants from the Legacy Fund for projects and initiatives that advance community development within the G78 3 postcode area. The fund was created from a £2 million donation from Neilston Development Trust following the sale of the Neilston Community Windfarm in 2017.
- **funding_amounts**: The maximum amount available for community grants is determined at the start of each financial year by the Board of Trustees. In 2026/27 this will be up to £40,000. The total amount available for distribution to the community in Year 1 was up to £100,000; in future years the amounts will vary but will probably be less. You can apply for grants appropriate to the size of your project.
- **deadlines**: Closing date: Friday 10th July 2026. The Board has paused acceptance of new grant applications until the end of the 2025/26 financial year. The Fund is planned to re-open for new Grant Applications in April 2026.
