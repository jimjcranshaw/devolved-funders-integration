### Conservation and Heritage Grants (St Andrews historic core)
- **description**: The Foundation awards grants to appropriate conservation projects in and around the historic core of St Andrews, in pursuance of its aim to conserve, improve and develop the historic character of the town. Grants are made alongside conservation and restoration works carried out under the Foundation's own auspices.
- **funding_amounts**: No individual grant amounts are published. The Foundation states it has 'given grants in excess of £400,000 to projects in and around the historic core of St Andrews' since 1999.
