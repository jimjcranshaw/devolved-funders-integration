### TBF Trust General Funding for Persecuted Christians
- **description**: TBF Trust forwards donations to churches and Christian ministries in places of pressure and persecution to fund their projects to help local believers in need. Some projects give practical help, some give spiritual help, and some strengthen the witness of the Church. Examples include monthly food parcels, church-run clinics, Bibles for Christian families, roofing sheets for shelter, and covering rent and electricity bills for Bible colleges.
