### An Ciste Infheistíochta Gaeilge (The Irish Language Community Investment Fund)
- **description**: A capital development fund established in 2010 to support Irish language community development. Its primary role is to assist major capital infrastructure projects that serve the social, economic and cultural needs of Irish language communities. The fund has been central to delivering a diverse range of community investment projects in partnership with various public bodies. A second phase of funding has been confirmed as part of the New Decade, New Approach agreement, with £4m from the British Government and €4.8m in matching funding from the Irish Government's Shared Island Initiative.
