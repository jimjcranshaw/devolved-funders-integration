### Agnes Hunter Trust Grants Programme
- **description**: The Trust operates one Grants Programme (grants over £3,000) with two rounds per calendar year: a Spring/Summer round and an Autumn/Winter round. The Trust focuses on one key area of funding: offering grants to charities that support disabled people in Scotland.
- **funding_amounts**: Grants can offer support for 1, 2 or 3 years. Whilst most grants are between £3,000-£15,000/yr the Trust reserves a degree of flexibility on these award values. We do not award grants less than £3,000 in total.
- **deadlines**: The Trust operates two rounds of grants per calendar year: a Spring/Summer round – with applications submitted in late January/early February, and taken to the Board meeting in late May/ early June. An Autumn/Winter round – with applications submitted in August, and taken to the Board meeting in late November/early December. Each round is open for approximately 3 weeks to allow applicants to submit their form. The Autumn/Winter 2026 round of grants has closed as of 5pm Thursday 27 August. The next round will open in January 2027. Exact date TBC.
