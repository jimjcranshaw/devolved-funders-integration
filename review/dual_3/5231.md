### Charitable Funding Application
- **description**: The Rotary Club of Forres disburses charitable funds each year. The form below enables clubs, charities and community groups to apply for funding.
- **funding_amounts**: Our typical disbursement is between £200 and £500 - although occasionally more is awarded. Larger awards made as a result of fundraising events INCLUDES for causes such as Shelterbox, Children in Need, Archies' and Maggies Highland amongst many others.
- **deadlines**: Applications are (usually) dealt with in April and October each year but applications can be submitted year round for review! Deadlines are 03 April and 03 October for main disbursements.
