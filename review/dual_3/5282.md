### Research Funding Application
- **description**: The charity invites applications for research funding. The main object of the Heart Trust Fund is the financial support of the vitally important work of treating patients in the Medical Cardiology Centre of the Royal Victoria Hospital, and the equally important work of research into heart disease carried out there. The charity has funded a wide variety of projects at the Hospital for the improvement of the treatments available to patients and also important research projects.
