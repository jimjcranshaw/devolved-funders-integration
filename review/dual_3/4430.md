### Financial Assistance for GPs and their Dependents
- **description**: The Cameron Fund is the GPs' own charity and the only medical benevolent charity that helps GPs and their families in times of financial need. It provides financial support to GPs, former GPs, and their dependents who are experiencing hardship or distress.
