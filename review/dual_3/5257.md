## Extracted opportunities (2)
### Annual Means-Tested Grant
- **description**: Annual means-tested grants to deserving widows and families, including mothers and sisters of freemasons who have been a member of a lodge in the Province of Down outside a six mile radius of Belfast.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified on the website. Assistance is by annual means tested grants as the Committee may find best suited to the necessities of the applicant.

### Bereavement Grant
- **description**: A one-off grant of £500 paid to the widow of a deceased Brother when a Lodge is bereaved by a member.
- **funding_amounts**: £500
- **deadlines**: No specific deadline. Lodges are encouraged to act speedily so that the widow receives the grant when perhaps she most needs it.
