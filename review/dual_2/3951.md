### VACMA Scotland: Grants for Artists & Makers Across Scotland (Visual Artist and Craft Maker Awards)
- **description**: Bursaries of £500 and £1000 now available. Visual artists and craft makers across Scotland are invited to apply for grants of up to £1000 to support their creative practice, develop new skills and take the next step in their professional journey. This funding is made possible by The National Lottery through Creative Scotland, working in partnership with 16 Visual Artist and Craft Maker Awards (VACMA) schemes covering 23 local authority areas. In the South of Scotland the scheme is delivered in partnership with Live Borders Arts & Creativity and Dumfries & Galloway Council providing match funding with additional support from DG Unlimited and Upland.
- **funding_amounts**: Bursaries of £500 and £1000; grants of up to £1000.
- **deadlines**: Autumn deadline: 5pm, 20 October 2026. Winter deadline: 5pm, 2 February 2027.

### Regional Cultural Fund
- **description**: The Regional Cultural Fund 2025/2026 is now closed. The fund was offered by Dumfries and Galloway Council to make grants of up to £4000 available to enhance, improve and promote community-led creative and cultural programmes and facilities in Dumfries and Galloway. The fund provided an ideal opportunity for arts, heritage and community organisations across the region to take forward work that could not otherwise be financed – to elevate their work and programmes. The Fund supported arts, creative and heritage organisations to diversify their work and programmes. The Fund supported projects that clearly contributed to the resilience of museums, galleries and cultural venues across Dumfries and Galloway whilst working towards achieving the aims, objectives and priorities of the Cultural Strategy for Dumfries and Galloway. The total fund was £44,000. The fund was fully funded by the UK government through the UK Shared Prosperity Fund.
- **funding_amounts**: Grants of up to £4000. The total fund was £44,000.
- **deadlines**: The Regional Cultural Fund 2025/2026 is now closed.
