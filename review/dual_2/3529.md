### General Grants Programme
- **description**: The Trust distributes grants to selected organisations promoting Scottish literature on an annual basis. The Trustees meet approximately three times a year to consider grant applications. Over the years since the Trust was set up it has made donations in excess of £135,000. Recent awards have included the Craigmillar Literacy Trust, the Scottish Poetry Library and the Borders Book Festival.
