## Extracted opportunities (2)
### Grants for Analogue Photography
- **description**: Grants to support photographers undertaking analogue photographic projects which promote, develop and practise analogue photography, and encourage learning about it.
- **funding_amounts**: You can apply for a grant of between £500 and £1,500. We can fund up to 100% of costs, but normally expect our contribution to be matched either by other funding or in-kind contributions.
- **deadlines**: Applications open: 16th June. Closing date: 30th September. Applications received after 30th September will not be considered, nor will they be held over to the following year.

### Grants for Community Photography
- **description**: Grants to support projects which use photography for community benefit by improving the mental health and well-being of children and young people under 25.
- **funding_amounts**: You can apply for a grant of between £500 and £1,500. We can fund up to 100% of costs, but normally expect our contribution to be matched either by other funding or in-kind contributions.
- **deadlines**: Applications open: 16th June. Closing date: 30th September. Applications received after 30th September will not be considered, nor will they be held over to the following year.
