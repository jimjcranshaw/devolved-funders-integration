### Postcode Education Trust Grant Funding
- **description**: The Trust supports charities and good causes with the purpose of providing education, particularly in emergency situations. It operates its own society lottery and receives all of its funding from the players of Postcode Lottery. As more players join Postcode Lottery, more funds are distributed to Postcode Education Trust and made available for the Trust to award to good causes.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified on the website. The Trust receives funds from the players of Postcode Lottery and distributes them as grants.
- **deadlines**: No specific deadlines are mentioned. The Trust proactively approaches charities when an opportunity arises.
