### The Ewe Hill 16 Fund
- **description**: An annual fund from ScottishPower Renewables of at least £184,000 (index linked) distributed to worthwhile projects and initiatives which benefit the people living and working in the area of East Dumfriesshire.
- **funding_amounts**: The fund will receive an annual contribution from ScottishPower Renewables of at least £184,000 (index linked over the operational life).
- **deadlines**: 31st January (for applications to be decided at the March Board meeting. Projects should not start before 1st April.) 31st July (for applications to be decided at the September meeting. Projects should not start before 1st October.)

### The Ewe Hill 6 Fund
- **description**: An annual fund from ScottishPower Renewables of at least £69,000 (index linked) shared between five local community groups for distribution to worthwhile projects to benefit the people who live and work in these communities. Each Group manages its own share of this Fund.
- **funding_amounts**: The fund will receive an annual contribution from ScottishPower Renewables of at least £69,000 (index linked over the operational life).

### The Solwaybank Windfarm Community Benefit Fund
- **description**: An annual fund from Solwaybank Energy Ltd of at least £60,000 (index linked) to contribute to community activities in specified areas. The fund is currently closed to new applications due to funds being fully allocated, but is hoped to reopen in October 2026.
- **funding_amounts**: The total amount of funding expected over the lifetime of the fund will be £1.5 million. The fund will receive an annual contribution from Solwaybank Energy Ltd of at least £60,000 (index linked over the operational life).
- **deadlines**: Currently closed. It is hoped that the Fund will reopen in October 2026, with a deadline for applications of 31 January 2027.

### Solwaybank Small Grants Fund
- **description**: A sub-fund of the Solwaybank Windfarm Community Benefit Fund, introduced from 1st December 2022. Further information is available in documents on the website.

### Solwaybank Education and Training Fund
- **description**: A sub-fund of the Solwaybank Windfarm Community Benefit Fund, introduced from 1st December 2022. Further information is available in documents on the website.
