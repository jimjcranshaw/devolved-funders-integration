## Extracted opportunities (2)
### Small Grant
- **description**: A grant for projects with a total cost below £5,000. This is one of two funding levels offered by Muaitheabhal Community Trust.
- **funding_amounts**: Under £5k. Grant amounts: Up to £50,000 per project. (Higher awards may be considered for exceptional applications with strong community benefit.) We typically fund up to 70% of project costs, with your organisation contributing at least 5% (cash or in-kind).
- **deadlines**: Applications are reviewed monthly.

### Large Grant
- **description**: A grant for projects with a total cost above £5,000 but less than £50,000. This is one of two funding levels offered by Muaitheabhal Community Trust.
- **funding_amounts**: Above £5k but less than £50k. Grant amounts: Up to £50,000 per project. (Higher awards may be considered for exceptional applications with strong community benefit.) We typically fund up to 70% of project costs, with your organisation contributing at least 5% (cash or in-kind).
- **deadlines**: Applications are reviewed monthly.
