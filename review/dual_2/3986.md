### Postcode International Trust Grant Funding
- **description**: The Trust supports charities and good causes with the purpose of supporting the creation of sustainable solutions that alleviate poverty, hunger and enable food security. The Trust is proactive in approaching charities and, as funds grow, will thoroughly research and select potential beneficiaries who will be invited to apply.
- **funding_amounts**: No specific grant amounts are listed. Funding is derived from the Trust's society lottery proceeds. In respect of the 2025 calendar year, 32% of the total ticket sales of the Trust's lotteries were applied to the purposes of the Trust, with a minimum of 30% applied to the purposes of the Trust.
- **deadlines**: No specific deadlines are listed. The Trust is proactive in approaching charities and invites them to apply when an opportunity arises.
