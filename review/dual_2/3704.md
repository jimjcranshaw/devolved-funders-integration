### Sports Scholarships
- **description**: The Benzies Foundation aims to provide scholarships to individuals or teams in Scotland, which will allow them to participate in their chosen sports - either just for one event (such as a tournament) or for a term of ongoing participation. You may require funding for equipment, for tuition or for travel costs to attend your sport for example.

### Sports Facilities
- **description**: The Benzies Foundation aims to provide funding for sporting facilities to be built in communities who require such facilities to advance public participation in sport. This could include, for example, building football pitches, tennis courts, or swimming pools. Please also apply if you have any other proposals that you believe we may be interested in providing funding for.
