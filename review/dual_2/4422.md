## Extracted opportunities (4)
### Core Charity Funding
- **description**: Multi-year funding and capacity-building support for a small network of charities focused on supporting young people growing up in and around Pilton and Muirhouse. Core Charities are selected for the different roles they play in responding to a wide range of needs, challenges, and circumstances in the area.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified. Around 50% of KPE4's funding is invested in a group of 10 Core Charities.
- **deadlines**: Reviewed and selected every two to three years.

### Joint Collaborative Approaches
- **description**: Funding for collaborative proposals where two or more charities wish to work together to better meet the needs of young people in the area.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified.
- **deadlines**: Reviewed on an ad hoc basis.

### Non-Core Charity Funding
- **description**: Funding for other organisations working directly with young people locally, including one-off projects and targeted initiatives intended to address specific issues and improve outcomes.
- **funding_amounts**: Requests for more than £10,000 are unlikely to be considered.
- **deadlines**: Applications are reviewed every one to two months.

### School-Recommended Support
- **description**: A separate fund that allows local schools to recommend charities and organisations for support.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified.
- **deadlines**: Not specified.
