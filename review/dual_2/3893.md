### Station Grants (Enrichment Grant Programme)
- **description**: Offering Station and Unit level support to enable serving personnel to access sport and physical activities. The programme supports personnel on a larger scale, traditionally awarding bids to stations and units to benefit the maximum number of personnel.
- **funding_amounts**: Small to medium grant applications (up to £5,000). Large grants (up to £50,000).
- **deadlines**: Applications may be submitted throughout the year. Small to medium grant applications (up to £5,000) are reviewed for decision on a monthly basis. Large grants (up to £50,000) are subject to review by our Grants Committee at one of three meetings scheduled throughout the year. Deadlines for completed applications for large grants: 13 February 2026, 15 May 2026, 11 September 2026.
