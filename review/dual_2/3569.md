### Green Community Grants Programme
- **description**: A new funding partnership launched in 2025 with The Wildlife Trusts, supporting a range of small green groups across England, Scotland, and Wales. The programme offers unrestricted grants of up to £25,000, thanks to players of Postcode Lottery. It provides one-year flexible funding for not-for-profit groups.
- **funding_amounts**: Unrestricted grants of up to £25,000.
- **deadlines**: The fund will be open in 2026 for three application windows: 25 March to 22 April; 24 June to 15 July; 30 September to 21 October.
