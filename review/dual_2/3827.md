## Extracted opportunities (5)
### Main Grants
- **description**: One-off grants for UK-based charities and other organisations (including schools) carrying out marine conservation related activities. The Main Grant Fund is allocated twice a year.
- **funding_amounts**: For grants between £500 and £2,500.
- **deadlines**: Two grant rounds per year. Deadlines for applications are 31st March and 30th September each year.

### Small Grants
- **description**: One-off grants for UK-based charities and other organisations (including schools) carrying out marine conservation related activities. Apply any time and get a quick decision.
- **funding_amounts**: For grants of up to £500.
- **deadlines**: Apply any time and get a quick decision.

### Scottish Learning Fund
- **description**: Supports community-based groups in Scotland who are involved in marine conservation to learn, share knowledge, network and develop skills with other groups. We're especially keen to support early-stage and / or small groups to grow and learn from others. This fund aims to directly address travel challenges.
- **deadlines**: The fund is always open - you can apply any time.

### Innovation Fund 2026
- **description**: The fund's unique emphasis is on supporting ideas that are new and/or experimental solutions in the field of marine conservation.
- **deadlines**: The closing date for applications is 30th April 2026.

### Coastal Fountain Fund 2026
- **description**: The fund aims to protect coastal habitats from the harmful effects of single-use plastic water bottles by supporting communities to purchase water refill fountains for busy or environmentally significant coastal areas.
- **funding_amounts**: Grants of up to £2,500 are available, per applicant, towards the cost of a fountain.
- **deadlines**: The closing date for applications is 28th February 2026.
