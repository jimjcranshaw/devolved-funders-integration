### NCCBC General Grant Funding
- **description**: NCCBC administers grant funding from ScottishPower Renewable's Dersalloch Windfarm and other sources that fit with the company and its charitable status. Funding is designated for the North Carrick area of South Ayrshire.
- **funding_amounts**: No specific amounts are listed on the website. Funds awarded range from £610 to £72,604.
- **deadlines**: 15th December (for February Board meeting), 15th March (for May Board meeting), 15th June (for August Board meeting), 1st September (for November Board meeting). Applications are reviewed quarterly at NCCBC's board meetings in February, May, August and November, with a cut off point 3 weeks prior to the meetings.
