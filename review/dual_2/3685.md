### The Edwin Morgan Poetry Award
- **description**: A national competition designed to identify and support the development of the best published or unpublished collection(s) of poetry by a young Scottish poet, or poets, under the age of thirty, according to the terms of the will of the late national poet, Edwin George Morgan. The Trust ensures that talented poets have a focus for extending the scope of their writing through national competition, publication and mentoring.
