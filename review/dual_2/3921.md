### Emma Cameron Memorial Trust Grants
- **description**: The Trust provides grants to charities that offer music therapy to disadvantaged people. The purpose of the Trust is to keep Emma Cameron's passionate and caring spirit alive by supporting projects and charities that benefit others and reflect her interests. The Trust is a small charity that allocates grants annually to selected charities.
- **funding_amounts**: In 2024, grants of £2,000 (to Nordoff Robbins) and £1,500 (to the Prince and Princess of Wales Hospice) were awarded. Annual expenditure on grants has ranged from £0 to £4,080 in recent years.
- **deadlines**: No specific deadlines are stated on the website. Grants appear to be allocated annually, with announcements typically made in December.
