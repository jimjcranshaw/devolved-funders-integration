### Carrick Futures Community Benefit Fund
- **description**: Carrick Futures supports community projects in South Carrick, South Ayrshire. The fund supports projects primarily located within or directly benefiting one or more of the seven community council areas of Ballantrae, Colmonell & Lendalfoot, Barr, Barrhill, Dailly, Pinmore & Pinwherry and Girvan & District. The fund aims to support and strengthen communities across South Carrick, supporting activities and opportunities that create thriving places where people want to live, work, raise their families and grow old.
- **funding_amounts**: Small grants: £1,000 - £5,000; Standard grants: £5,001 - £50,000; Large grants: £50,001 and above; Micro grants: up to £1,000 (administered by Community Councils). Awards can be multi-year in nature, normally up to a maximum of 3 years.
- **deadlines**: 2026 Application Deadlines: 23rd February (Round 1) - Outcome expected Late April 2026; 25th May (Round 2) - Outcome expected Late July 2026; 21st September (Round 3) - Outcome expected Late November 2026; 4th November (Round 4) - Outcome expected Late January 2027.
