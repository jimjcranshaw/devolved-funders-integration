### Alfred Dunhill Links Foundation Grant
- **description**: The Foundation receives charitable funds raised by the Alfred Dunhill Links Championship and distributes those funds through grants to local and international charities and other benevolent bodies selected by the Trustees in support of worthy causes. The Foundation funds both on-going Programme Delivery and low scale Capital costs.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified on the website. Grants vary and in special circumstances the Trustees may make recurring grants.
- **deadlines**: Expression of Interest Forms must be received at the offices of the Foundation by July 1st each year. Application Forms must be received by August 1st each year. Trustees meet annually prior to the Alfred Dunhill Links Championship in October.
