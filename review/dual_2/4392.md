### Localgiving Grant Search and Matching Service
- **description**: Localgiving provides a grant-finding and matching service giving access to a database of 2,500+ live UK grants, ranked by how well they fit a charity's work. It also offers AI-assisted bid writing and application drafting. This is a signposting/matching service rather than a single grant pot administered by Localgiving itself.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified for individual grants. Localgiving states '£7m+ in grants distributed since 2024' and '£70m raised for local causes' across the platform.
- **deadlines**: Not specified - grants listed are described as 'live' and vary by funder.

### Localgiving Fundraising Platform and Gift Aid Service
- **description**: Localgiving provides fundraising pages for charities and CICs, enabling them to collect donations via a single shareable link and automatically claim Gift Aid (an extra 25% from HMRC) on eligible donations. This is a fundraising infrastructure service rather than a grant programme.
- **funding_amounts**: Not applicable - donations are raised directly from supporters. The website cites examples such as '£4.6k raised in 2026 so far' (Faith in Families) and 'Over £1.2m raised' (Cardiff Foodbank).
- **deadlines**: Not applicable - ongoing service.
