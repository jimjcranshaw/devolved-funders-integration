### Global's Make Some Noise - Small Charity Funding
- **description**: Global's Make Some Noise funds and champions projects delivered by small charities across the UK that are lifelines in their communities. The charity states: 'Every day, we fund and champion projects delivered by small charities across the UK that are lifelines in their communities - whether they're supporting families facing a life-limiting illness, someone struggling with their mental health, escaping domestic abuse, or trying to make ends meet. With your help, we give these projects the funding, tools and platform they need to keep going further, reaching more people when life gets really hard.'
