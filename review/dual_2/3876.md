## Extracted opportunities (6)
### Torrance Award
- **description**: Bursaries awarded to junior golfers who have been selected by Scottish Golf for the national team or squad. The bursaries are to meet subsistence and travel costs for participation in tournaments and events.

### Education and Coaching for Golf
- **description**: One bursary awarded each year to a junior golfer who wishes to study at a University or College to further their golf performance or pursue a career in golf.
- **funding_amounts**: One bursary per year. Amount not specified.

### Golf for Everyone
- **description**: Grants that support clubs and individuals to ensure that everyone has the opportunity to play golf in Scotland. This could include grants for disabled golfers to purchase OpenPlay membership, or to meet subsistence needs and travel costs for participation in G4D and EDGA tournaments.

### Unleash Your Drive Transport Fund
- **description**: Unleash Your Drive is a schools programme delivered in partnership with The Golf Foundation. The Trust offers schools the opportunity to apply for funding to aid the transition of school aged children from golf in the community or school setting, into their local club.

### Scottish Golf Disability Bursary
- **description**: A subsistence fund for golfers with a disability competing in the Scottish Open for Golfers with a Disability. The costs of travel and accommodation can be elevated for people with a disability, and this fund supports those requirements, enabling more golfers to access tournaments across Scotland.

### Roving Pro
- **description**: In partnership with Scottish Golf, the Trust provides match funding to golf clubs in specific locations, to enable them to participate in the Roving Pro initiative. This funding is utilised for rural and island clubs that fall within HS, KW and ZE postcodes to support the increased costs of coaching.
- **funding_amounts**: Match funding. Amount not specified.
