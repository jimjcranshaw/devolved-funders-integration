### Postcode Global Trust Grant Funding
- **description**: The Trust supports charities and good causes whose purposes are to save lives and improve health care systems. Funding is awarded to a selection of charities that meet the objectives of the Trust. The Trust is proactive in approaching charities and, as funds grow, will thoroughly research and select potential beneficiaries who will be invited to apply.
- **funding_amounts**: No specific grant amounts are listed on the website. Funding is derived from the Trust's society lottery, with 32% of total ticket sales in the 2025 calendar year applied to the purposes of the Trust. A minimum of 30% will be applied to the purposes of the Trust.
- **deadlines**: No specific deadlines are listed. The Trust is proactive in approaching charities and will invite them to apply when an opportunity arises.
