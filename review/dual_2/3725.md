### HE/FE Procurement Development Fund (HPDF) Main Grant
- **description**: The purpose of the HE/FE Procurement Development Fund is to grant awards to individuals and/or collective groups who are or may be involved in procurement within the university or college sectors to support and significantly enhance their procurement skills and/or procurement leadership skills. It provides funding support that will be beneficial to the individual, the institution and the sector, and offers financial support for a defined training activity/development opportunity in periodic offerings which the Trustees feel would encourage/bring an overall increase in procurement capability in the sectors.
- **funding_amounts**: The maximum funding level is £2000 applicable over a 2 year period. There are limits to how much one individual can apply for and be awarded from the fund, this being £2,000 over any two consecutive year period. Although a cap will be set for each round of funding, there will be no minimum value set for award.
- **deadlines**: There will be four Funding Rounds per academic year. Advertising of the deadlines etc shall be undertaken approximately three months before each deadline. The thirtythird round of funding is now closed from Friday 11 September. Applications will be considered by the Trustees on Tuesday 22 September 2026.

### HPDF Conference Support
- **description**: The HPDF Trustees have agreed to provide a contribution to a pre-agreed list of well attended conferences. This funding is for conference fees only.
- **funding_amounts**: Up to a maximum of £100 per person for Conference Fees up to a maximum of 5 people per institution.
- **deadlines**: Support for attending future conferences is temporarily suspended. Any updates to this situation will be posted on the website.
