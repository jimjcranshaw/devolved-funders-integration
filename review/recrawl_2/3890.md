### Mary's Mercury Trust Grants
- **description**: The trust provides grants/donations to charities and voluntary organisations, primarily focussed on Angus and Kincardineshire and with a particular interest in helping organisations overcome unexpected costs, e.g. expenditure required as a consequence of flood or fire damage. Trustees will assess the immediate need and long term prospects of organisations which come to their attention. This may involve site visits and other appropriate enquiries. Trustees will then determine whether or not to award a grant and any reporting requirements on a case by case basis.
