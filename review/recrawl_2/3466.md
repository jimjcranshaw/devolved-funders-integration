### Wilson Family Charitable Trust Grant Funding
- **description**: The charity was set up to encourage participation by younger people and youth organisations in activities and interests, principally sporting activities. The charity accepts applications from sporting organisations, youth organisations and individual young people seeking financial support to participate in events. The charity has supported a variety of groups to acquire equipment/sports kits/trophies etc in a variety of sports - athletics, football, curling, skiing and golf.
- **eligibility_criteria**: Applications accepted from sporting organisations, youth organisations and individual young people seeking financial support to participate in events.
- **beneficiary_groups**: Children or young people, Older People, People with disabilities or health problems, No specific group, or for the benefit of the community
