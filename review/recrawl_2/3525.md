### Lord Provost's Fund for Vulnerable Citizens
- **description**: Provides grants to individuals and institutions to help achieve its goals of preventing poverty, advancing education, citizenship and community development, and relieving hardship of vulnerable citizens of Glasgow.
- **eligibility_criteria**: The prevention or relief of poverty within the City of Glasgow; the advancement of education within the City of Glasgow; the advancement of citizenship and community development within the City of Glasgow; the relief of those living in the City of Glasgow in need by reason of age, ill health, disability, financial hardship or other disadvantage.
- **beneficiary_groups**: No specific group, or for the benefit of the community
