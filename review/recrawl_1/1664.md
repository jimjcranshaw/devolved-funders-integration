### Columba Charitable Trust Grants
- **description**: Provide grants to charitable organisations as agreed by the Trustees. Appeals from organisations are gathered and passed to the Trustees to consider. Once the grants are agreed the solicitors send cheques to the various successful applicants.
- **eligibility_criteria**: The purposes of the Trust shall, so far, and so far only, as the same are legally charitable, be:- (1) the relief of poverty; (2) the advancement of education; (3) the advancement of religion; and (4) such other purposes beneficial to the community as are exclusively charitable according to the Law of Scotland.
- **beneficiary_groups**: Other charities or voluntary bodies
