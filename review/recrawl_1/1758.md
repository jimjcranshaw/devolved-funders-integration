### Guy-Lockhart Charitable Trust Grant Funding
- **description**: The Guy-Lockhart Charitable Trust provides funding to benefit institutions set out in the Trust by the late Miss Guy and the late Miss Lockhart. The Trustees have discretion to add to the list originally set out. The charity's purposes include the prevention or relief of poverty, the advancement of education, the advancement of religion, the advancement of health, and the advancement of citizenship or community development.
- **eligibility_criteria**: Beneficiaries: Other charities or voluntary bodies. The charity is set up to benefit institutions set out in the Trust by the late Miss Guy and the late Miss Lockhart, with Trustees having discretion to add to the list originally set out.
