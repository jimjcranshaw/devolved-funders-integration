### Clark Foundation for Legal Education Grants
- **description**: The Foundation awards grants or scholarships to suitable persons to undertake courses of study in Scots Law or comparative legal systems or the law of the European Community or foreign languages or business management or undertake research into one or more aspects of Scots Law and/or its relationship with other legal systems or attend international student competitions and conferences. It also commissions and promotes or sponsors publications, holds or sponsors conferences, seminars, lectures, webinars, mock trials, role playing for events promoting legal education and practical demonstrations, provides facilities and equipment for study and research, endows chairs or lectureships of law, institutes and endows prizes and awards for scholarship and academic achievement, and generally undertakes or promotes activities that further the purposes of the Foundation.
- **eligibility_criteria**: The Foundation's purpose is to promote or advance the legal or the professional or the business education or training of persons practising law or involved (or intending to practise law or be so involved) in the administration of law in Scotland or elsewhere in the United Kingdom whether as solicitors or advocates or in any other capacity and persons studying or teaching law at universities or other institutions of higher education based in Scotland or anywhere else in the world, and to promote good citizenship and civic responsibility and for that purpose to advance the active understanding of the law by the general public (including primary and secondary students at schools and other learning institutions in Scotland).
- **beneficiary_groups**: Persons practising law or involved (or intending to practise law or be so involved) in the administration of law in Scotland or elsewhere in the United Kingdom whether as solicitors or advocates or in any other capacity; persons studying or teaching law at universities or other institutions of higher education based in Scotland or anywhere else in the world; the general public (including primary and secondary students at schools and other learning institutions in Scotland).
