### Gordon Leisure Projects Trust Grants
- **description**: The Trust gives grants to organisations within the area of the former Gordon District Council to further the charitable purposes. The charity's object is to advance the education of the public throughout Gordon District, by the provision of playing fields, pavilions and other recreational amenities and facilities.
- **eligibility_criteria**: Organisations within the area of the former Gordon District Council, to further the charitable purposes.
- **beneficiary_groups**: No specific group, or for the benefit of the community
