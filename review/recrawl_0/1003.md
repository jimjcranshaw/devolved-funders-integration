### William A Cargill Charitable Trust Grants
- **description**: The trustees apply the income of the trust for such charitable purposes as they in their absolute discretion may decide. Payments are made to or on behalf of any Hospitals, Institutions, Societies or others whose work in the opinion of the Trustees is likely to be beneficial to the community.
- **eligibility_criteria**: Payments are made to or on behalf of any Hospitals, Institutions, Societies or others whose work in the opinion of the Trustees is likely to be beneficial to the community. It is not the Trustees current policy to give grants to individuals.
- **beneficiary_groups**: Children or young people, Older People, People with disabilities or health problems, No specific group, or for the benefit of the community, Other charities or voluntary bodies
