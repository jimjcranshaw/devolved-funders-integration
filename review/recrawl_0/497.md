### Royal British Legion Scotland Kelso Branch Grants
- **description**: The charity is set up for the relief of poverty and assistance in welfare, for the elderly and infirm. It benefits other charities with similar charitable objectives through the provision of services and activities for members of the Legion, and also provides financial assistance and grants to individuals and organisations/charities by way of donations.
- **eligibility_criteria**: For the relief of poverty and assistance in welfare, for the elderly and infirm. To benefit other charities with similar charitable objectives. For ex-Service men and women in need by reason of age, ill-health, disability, financial hardship or other disadvantage.
- **beneficiary_groups**: Ex-Service men and women, the elderly and infirm, and organisations/charities with similar charitable objectives.
