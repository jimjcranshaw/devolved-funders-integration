### Ayrshire Yeomanry Trust Fund (Earl Of Carrick's Own)
- **description**: For general welfare purposes and without prejudice to said generality for the purpose of making Grants and annual payments to serving and ex-serving members of the Ayrshire (Earl of Carrick's Own) Yeomanry (herereinafter referred to as “The Regiment”) who are in need and to their wives, widows, children and dependants who may be in need.
- **eligibility_criteria**: Serving and ex-serving members of the Ayrshire (Earl of Carrick's Own) Yeomanry who are in need and their wives, widows, children and dependants who may be in need.
- **beneficiary_groups**: Other defined groups
