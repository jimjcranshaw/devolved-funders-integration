## Extracted opportunities (3)
### International Fellowships
- **description**: Applications are invited to the USF’s International Fellowships for urban scholars from the Global South. Each award will cover the cost of a visiting sabbatical period at a host university of the candidate’s choice, for the purpose of writing-up the candidate’s existing research findings in the form of publishable articles, book chapters, and/or books. The proposed work should be completed under the guidance of a chosen Mentor in the candidate’s field of study. Funding is available for a period ranging between three and nine months, and eligible research may cover any urban studies theme pertinent to a better understanding of urban realities within and across the Global South.
- **deadlines**: The deadline for applications to this funding has now passed. The scheme is expected to re-open in early 2027.

### Seminar Series Awards
- **description**: The USF Seminar Series Awards aim to support the generation of pioneering global research in all areas of urban studies. The Urban Studies Foundation (USF) seeks to support up to twelve awards in the 2024 round of the scheme, in each case up to a maximum of £20,000 per award (or up to £25,000 for proposals containing eligible training elements).
- **funding_amounts**: Up to a maximum of £20,000 per award (or up to £25,000 for proposals containing eligible training elements).
- **deadlines**: The deadline for applications to this funding has now passed.

### Urban Urgencies
- **description**: The Urban Studies Foundation (USF) is pleased to launch a new research funding initiative to seed-fund collaborative primary research proposals that engage with the theme Urban Urgencies. Up to six innovative projects will be awarded up to GBP £35,000 each. Project activities should begin within nine months of the application deadline, and are expected to last up to eighteen months. All proposals should be clearly situated and relevant to the broader academic field of urban studies, and their contribution to scholarly debate and dialogue in this field should be compelling and timely. Proposals must also be based upon an active partnership with at least one non-academic organisation.
- **funding_amounts**: Up to six innovative projects will be awarded up to GBP £35,000 each.
- **deadlines**: The deadline for applications to this grant has now passed.
