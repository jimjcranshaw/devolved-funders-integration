### Funding Awards
- **description**: The Trust provides financial awards to support the learning and ventures of traditional musicians and to promote piping and other traditional Scottish music. Applications can be made by any person, living or working in Scotland, who is finding that their financial circumstances are making it difficult to progress fully in their chosen field.
- **funding_amounts**: The website does not specify minimum or maximum grant amounts. Applicants must provide details of the funding amount requested and a breakdown of likely expenditure. Historic awards range from £120 to £3,500.
- **deadlines**: The closing date for applications is Friday 27 November 2026.
