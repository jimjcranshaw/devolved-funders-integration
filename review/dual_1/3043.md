### Coalfields Community Grants - Capital Grant
- **description**: The Coalfields Community Capital Grant in Wales can award capital funding from £500 up to £7000 and is funded by Welsh Government. The aim of this programme is to support community and voluntary organisations, who manage community facilities, to access funding required to: safeguard; maintain; and/or improve their community facility.
- **funding_amounts**: £500-£7,000
- **deadlines**: Rolling annual programme.

### Breaking Barriers Fund
- **description**: The aim of the funding is for the development of services for people affected by cancer in the Cwm Taf Morgannwg University Health Board area (Merthyr Tydfil, Rhondda Cynon Taf and Bridgend valleys) where there is a clear evidence of need and clear, measurable outcomes. The fund is responsive to various needs and supports both capital and revenue costs.
