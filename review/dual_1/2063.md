### Cancer Research Funding
- **description**: The Scottish Cancer Foundation provides funding for cancer research in Scotland. The charity's object is to promote the reduction of deaths in Scotland caused by cancer, to encourage and promote standards of excellence in research into the causes, methods of prevention and treatment of cancer in Scotland; and to advance the education, knowledge and awareness of the public in general of the causes of cancer and potential methods of prevention.
