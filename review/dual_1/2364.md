### General Charitable Donations (Rotary Club of West Fife Charitable Trust Fund)
- **description**: The charity raises funds through donations and fundraising activities and distributes them as donations to other charities and groups with similar aims, both locally and internationally. It also holds youth events to encourage and support local children in competitions run by Rotary UK. Previous donations have been made to local groups including the Limekilns and Charlestown Gala Committees, Therapets, Dunfermline Sounds and Shiresmill Therapy, and the charity has joined the Specsort project run by the Rotary Club of York Ainsty which takes pairs of unwanted and redundant spectacles, grades them and sends them to Africa.
