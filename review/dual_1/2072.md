### Kincardineshire Educational Trust Grants
- **description**: The Kincardineshire Educational Trust was set up for educational purposes for the residents of the former County of Kincardine. The charity awards grants to individuals, clubs, societies and organisations for help with the costs of further education, practical experiences of trade, student apprenticeships, travel grants and sports facilities.
