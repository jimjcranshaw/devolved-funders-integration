### NHS Forth Valley Endowment Funds
- **description**: The Charity's main objective is 'the advancement of health' including the prevention or relief of sickness, disease or human suffering within Forth Valley. The Charity furthers its charitable objective through the provision of patient welfare & amenities, the purchase of equipment and services, the development of medical research, the support of staff educational opportunities and a number of other social investments. The Charity also provides grants to other non-profit organisations in furtherance of the objectives.
