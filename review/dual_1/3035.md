### Travel Grants
- **description**: These grants provide funding for members to attend scientific conferences, meetings, or courses to present their work or to further their knowledge.
- **funding_amounts**: Up to £1,000.
- **deadlines**: Applications are considered throughout the year, but must be submitted at least 8 weeks before the event.
