### Conference Support
- **description**: Grants to support philosophy events organised in Scotland, funded by income from sales of the Philosophical Quarterly.

### SPA Centenary Fellowship
- **description**: A special grant that enables one of the departments to host a visit by a distinguished philosopher for a period of between one and six months. The grant may also be used to host shorter visits by several philosophers.
- **deadlines**: Not specified on the website. The fellowship is awarded annually.
