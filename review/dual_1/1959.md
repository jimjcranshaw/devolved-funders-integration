### Major Beneficiary Funding
- **description**: Highland Cross seeks to fund charities and incorporated organisations that work for the benefit of the people of Highland who are disadvantaged through medical or social causes. Major beneficiaries are expected to contribute to the successful organisation of the event by contributing £1,000 towards the costs.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified for major beneficiaries. The overall total for years 1983 to 2024 is £6,248,189. In 2024, the total distributed was £346,288.
- **deadlines**: 30 September each year.

### Small Grants
- **description**: Applications for smaller amounts of assistance should also be made by 30 September each year, and if surplus funds are available from that year's event, a distribution may be made thereafter. The smallest grant ever given was for just under £60.
- **funding_amounts**: Smaller amounts of assistance. The smallest grant ever given was for just under £60.
- **deadlines**: 30 September each year.
