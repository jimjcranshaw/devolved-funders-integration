### Community ENABLE Funding (CEF) Programme
- **description**: Fair, flexible finance for businesses that can't always access mainstream lending and seeking finance of £25,001 - £125,000. This is a pilot phase of SIS's accreditation under the British Business Bank's Community ENABLE Funding (CEF) programme, and the first time SIS has been able to provide funds to limited companies, sole traders or partnerships.
- **funding_amounts**: Loans from £25,001 to £125,000.
- **deadlines**: The pilot will run until December 2026.
