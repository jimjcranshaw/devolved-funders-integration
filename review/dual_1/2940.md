### Andy Jackson Fund for Access Grant
- **description**: The Fund provides funding for projects to make it easier for paddlers to get on Scotland's rivers, canals, lochs and sea shores. It considers any project in Scotland that helps paddlers get on the water.
- **funding_amounts**: Grants are normally up to £1000, but the fund is open to persuasion for larger grants especially if it will help release more funds.
- **deadlines**: No specific deadlines are mentioned. Applicants must wait for the outcome of their application before starting their project.
