### Glens of Foudland Community Benefit Fund
- **description**: The Glens of Foudland Wind Farm Community Fund was set up in 2006 to ensure the local community benefited from the development of the wind farm. Annual donations will be made throughout the working life of the wind farm to support local good causes within the community council areas of Strathbogie, Tap O'Noth and Bennachie. The Glens of Foudland Wind Farm Community Trust has been set up as an independent body and will make all decisions with regard to funding requests.
- **deadlines**: Applications for grants will be considered at meetings throughout the year. The deadline for applications is 14 days prior to each meeting. Meeting dates and deadlines: 12th November 2026 (deadline 29th October 2026), 11th February 2027 (deadline 28th January 2027), 13th May 2027 (deadline 29th April 2027), 12th August 2027 (deadline 29th July 2027), 11th November 2027 (deadline 28th October 2027).
