## Extracted opportunities (4)
### Entertainment Grant
- **description**: This grant is available in order to enable groups to put on events in Westray. The Trust Board recognises that the cost of travelling to Westray and freight costs could be seen as prohibitive for event organisation, therefore funding is available to a maximum of £500 per event which could be used for costs such as the travel and accommodation costs of travelling acts, freight costs of equipment, decoration, catering or room hire.
- **funding_amounts**: Maximum of £500 per event.
- **deadlines**: Applications must be with the team before the 30th of September 2026 (for the next batch of Community Grant applications, which may apply to all Community Turbine Funds).

### Community Grant
- **description**: This grant is available for one-off project costs and is to be used to supplement existing funding for projects not be the sole funder. Grants will fund a maximum of 80% of the total cost of the project. Community Grants will be available until June 2027 or until the pot empties.
- **funding_amounts**: Maximum of 80% of the total cost of the project.
- **deadlines**: Applications must be with the team before the 30th of September 2026. Community Grants will be available until June 2027 or until the pot empties.

### Investment Budget Grant
- **description**: This grant is one of the five different grant budgets provided by the Trust from the community turbine income. Further details are not provided on the website.

### Westray Community Business Loan Scheme
- **description**: Westray Development Trust will consider applications for a community business loan for a new or existing business venture that: Is owned and operated in Westray. Maintains and/or creates new employment in Westray. Supports the viability of Westray.
