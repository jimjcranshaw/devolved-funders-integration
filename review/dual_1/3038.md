### The Anchor Foundation Grant Funding
- **description**: The Anchor Foundation supports Christian charities concerned with social inclusion, particularly through ministries of healing and the arts. The Foundation considers applications for projects in the UK or overseas.
- **funding_amounts**: The maximum grant provided is £12,000.
- **deadlines**: Deadlines for receipt of applications are 31st January and 31st July. Applications are considered at twice yearly Trustee meetings, usually held in April & October.
