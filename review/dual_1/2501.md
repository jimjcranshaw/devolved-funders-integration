### Nurturing Talent Programme
- **description**: The ICAS Foundation is an independent charity supporting talented young people from low-income backgrounds into the accountancy profession, through bursaries, mentoring and real career opportunities. Applying to the ICAS Foundation Nurturing Talent Programme means committing to more than a bursary. If you're accepted, you'll be matched with a mentor, given financial support for the length of your studies, and access to internships and career opportunities most students don't get.

### Additional Support Fund
- **description**: One-off financial support for current students facing particular challenges.
