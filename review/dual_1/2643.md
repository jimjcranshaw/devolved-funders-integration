### Robina Goodlad Memorial Trust Small Grants
- **description**: The Trust makes a small number of grants to support activities that serve its purposes of social justice, relief of poverty and community development within deprived areas in Scotland. Where possible, the Trust prefers to support newly started community groups or activities, or new ventures by existing groups, that are not covered by any existing funding.
- **funding_amounts**: At present, we will consider giving a maximum of £8,000 in assistance, though we expect most awards to be below £4,000.
- **deadlines**: No specific deadlines stated.
