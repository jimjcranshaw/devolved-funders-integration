### Community Grant
- **description**: LDT Community Grants are available for local charity or not-for-profit organisations based in Lesmahagow, Blackwood, Kirkmuirhill, Draffan, Boghead and Auchenheath.
- **funding_amounts**: Grant Allocations will be up to £500. Only 1 Allocation can be made to an organisation in a 12 month period.
- **deadlines**: 1st March, 1st June, 1st September, 1st December

### Young Achievers Award
- **description**: Lesmahagow Development Trust (LDT) Young Achievers Grants are available to young people still at school who live in Lesmahagow, Blackwood, Kirkmuirhill, Draffan, Boghead and Auchenheath. The grant purpose is to financially assist the applicant in attending/entering competitions both nationally and internationally in an activity for which they have ALREADY shown a significant and recognised level of aptitude.
- **funding_amounts**: There will be a maximum award of £500. Only One Grant Award can be made to a young person in any 12 month period.
- **deadlines**: 1st March, 1st June, 1st September, 1st December

### Dalquandy Community Fund
- **description**: LDT are administering on behalf of Coalburn, a Community Benefit Grant from the Dalquandy Wind farm. Grants will be available to support environmental, renewable energy, energy efficiency, sustainable development, charitable, educational, community, sport and recreation, and general community amenity.
- **funding_amounts**: There is no upper limit on the application amount. Guidance on holding monies back one year for a future project will be considered on a case by case basis.
- **deadlines**: Application deadline end June - Decision made by end July, Application deadline end August - Decision made by end September, Application deadline end November - Decision made by end of January, Application deadline end February - Decision made by end March
