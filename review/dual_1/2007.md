### Venture Philanthropy Partnerships
- **description**: The Hunter Foundation (THF) is a proactive venture philanthropy that seeks to invest in determining model solutions, in partnership with others, to troubling systemic issues relating to poverty reduction and educational enablement. The model is to find innovative partners, disrupters, fund them against pre-agreed Key Performance Indicators whilst always remembering the customer, the individuals the innovation is there to serve.
- **funding_amounts**: No specific funding amounts are listed for open applications. Historical examples include a £5m endowment for The Hunter Centre for Entrepreneurship and a joint £2m partnership with BBC Children in Need.
- **deadlines**: No deadlines are listed on the website.
