## Extracted opportunities (4)
### Research and Quality Improvement Grants
- **description**: Small research grants up to the value of £5000. Preference is given to preliminary pump priming studies or quality improvement work, patient-related projects and in particular, applications from early career investigators.
- **funding_amounts**: Up to £5000
- **deadlines**: Applications for research and quality improvement grants are currently closed.

### Sabbaticals
- **description**: The Society offers grants of up to £5000 to allow members to undertake a sabbatical study. Previous grants have allowed members to travel to specialist centres to learn new clinical or research techniques.
- **funding_amounts**: Up to £5000
- **deadlines**: Applications for sabbatical grants are currently closed.

### Travel Grants
- **description**: Trainees who are selected to give an oral presentation at the Annual Meeting are eligible for a travel grant of up to £500 to allow them to travel to present their work at another meeting.
- **funding_amounts**: Up to £500
- **deadlines**: Applications for travel grants are currently closed.

### Fitzgerald Peel Prize
- **description**: The Society offers a cash prize of £250 for the trainee adjudged to have made the best presentation from the oral presentations to the Society’s AGM. Abstracts of the oral presentations including the prizewinning entry are published in the Scottish Medical Journal. There are additional prizes for poster presentations awarded at the annual meeting also.
- **funding_amounts**: £250
- **deadlines**: No separate deadline; tied to the Annual Meeting abstract submission deadline (9am, Wednesday 13 May 2026 for the 2026 meeting).
