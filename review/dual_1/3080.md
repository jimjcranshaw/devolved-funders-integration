## Extracted opportunities (3)
### BSc/MSc Student Bursary
- **description**: AFES offers a student bursary to BSc & MSc students who have a significant petrophysics component in their studies. The successful students will be invited to give a brief presentation at an AFES evening event.
- **funding_amounts**: The amount currently available is £1500 per student.
- **deadlines**: BSc/MSc funding applications are open from September to December.

### PhD Student Funding
- **description**: AFES offers partial funding for students who are undertaking a PhD in Petrophysics, Geology, Geophysics, Reservoir Engineering or Petroleum Engineering.
- **funding_amounts**: The amount is £3000 per student per year.
- **deadlines**: PhD funding applications are open from September to December.

### AFES – University Funding
- **description**: AFES has money available for funding equipment, wider projects, courses or field trips that have a significant petrophysical and/or STEM (Science Technology Engineering Mathematics) component to them.
- **funding_amounts**: Not specified.
- **deadlines**: Funding is available throughout the year.
