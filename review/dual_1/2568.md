### Alice McCosh Trust Grant
- **description**: The Trust awards one-off grants in the region of £1000. Grants could be used, for example, to cover the cost of fieldwork undertaken as part of a research project, or the costs of producing materials that enhance knowledge and understanding of natural history and the environment, or that encourage people to engage with the environment and take action to protect it.
- **funding_amounts**: One-off grants in the region of £1000.
- **deadlines**: Applications are open until the 30th November 2026. Applications should be emailed between 1st October and 30th November each year.
