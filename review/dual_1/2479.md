### Rotary Club Of Perth Charitable Trust Fund – General Grants and Donations
- **description**: The moneys specified in the schedule hereto belong to the Rotary Club of Perth in the County of Perthshire Scotland (hereinafter called "the Club") and it is proposed that they shall be devoted by the Club to charitable objects and purposes. The charity is a Rotary Club, set up to provide service to others, and does this by following the Objects of Rotary International. It holds social and fundraising events, makes grants and donations, and does voluntary work in Perth and Environs.
