### AEB Charitable Trust General Grants
- **description**: The AEB Charitable Trust presently makes grants to charities in support of four main areas. The Trustees' policy is to make regular donations to selected organisations whose policies fall within the Trust Deed. This can be in the method of a one off donation or multi-year commitments made by the Trustees.
- **funding_amounts**: The Trustees typically award grants of between £1,000 and £10,000. Larger grants are available in very limited circumstances at the Trustees' discretion. The Trustees will consider multi-year commitments.
- **deadlines**: Applications should reach us by email or in hard copy no later than 30th November. Trustees meet once a year in Spring to decide on grant applications for the year and normally pay grants prior to 31st March.
