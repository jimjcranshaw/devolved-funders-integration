### Energise
- **description**: Energise is an innovative, trusting, and bold grants programme enabling grassroots charities who are alive to the real issues on the ground to grow, and support more vulnerable people. It provides unrestricted funding.
- **funding_amounts**: Energise has two funding tiers based on the income of charities: charities with an income of below £50k are eligible for £20k (£10k per year over 2 years); charities with an income of £50k-£150k are eligible for £40k (£20k per year over 2 years).
- **deadlines**: Round 1: Opens Noon Monday 28th September, Closes Noon Wednesday 7th October, Charities advised of outcome by Mid December 2026, Funding awarded Mid January 2027. Round 2: Opens May 2027 (Date TBC), Closes May 2027 (Date TBC), Charities advised of outcome by End August 2027, Funding awarded End September 2027. Round 3: Opens September 2027 (Date TBC), Closes September 2027 (Date TBC), Charities advised of outcome by End November 2027, Funding awarded End December 2027.

### Enable
- **description**: Enable is an innovative, joint grants programme with Lloyds Banking Group aimed at boosting community volunteering. Funding will be provided to charities to support projects delivered through volunteers, with Lloyds Banking Group’s colleagues across Scotland volunteering their time and skills to help deliver the projects and support the charities.
- **funding_amounts**: Charities can apply for a 3 year grant of £30,000 - £90,000 (£10,000 - £30,000 per annum).
- **deadlines**: CLOSED FOR 2026.

### Matched Giving
- **description**: The Matched Giving Programme encourages Lloyds Banking Group colleagues in Scotland to get involved in the voluntary sector and to help fundraise through personal participation in events or give their time freely to support the work of charities throughout Scotland. Our Matched Giving Programme ensures our funding reaches charities at a grass-roots level across Scotland, creating a lasting positive impact in local Scottish communities.
- **funding_amounts**: We will match every pound they raise or donate £10 per hour of voluntary time given, to a maximum of £500 for fundraising events and/or £500 for voluntary time given, provided that the claim falls within the rules of the scheme and the charity is eligible for funding. Every permanent Lloyds Banking Group colleague in Scotland is entitled to claim up to £1000 in each calendar year.
- **deadlines**: The funding year runs from January to December.
