## Extracted opportunities (3)
### Domestic Property (House) Grant
- **description**: A grant to offset high electricity connection costs for individual domestic properties. The Trust was established to provide help to customers faced with high charges for an electricity connection.
- **funding_amounts**: For domestic customers a grant of up to 50% of the connection cost may be awarded. The amount of grant assistance offered will take account of what the Trustees consider to be a reasonable connection charge. The level of award granted will be at the sole discretion of the Trustees.
- **deadlines**: Thursday 10 September 2026 (for meeting on Thursday 8 October 2026). Thursday 10 December 2026 (for meeting on Thursday 21 January 2027). Monday 1 March 2027 (for meeting on Thursday 29 April 2027). Monday 14 June 2027 (for meeting on Thursday 22 July 2027). Monday 13 September 2027 (for meeting on Thursday 21 October 2027). Applications must be received by 12 noon on the deadline date.

### Community Owned Project Grant
- **description**: A grant to offset high electricity connection costs for community projects which are not profit distributing.
