### General Grant
- **description**: Grants can be applied for a variety of purposes where the Trustees will then use their discretion to decide which grants to approve. Preference will be given to grants which fall into one of the following criteria: Facilitating the education of school children attending Scottish schools in agricultural practices and farming; Providing scholarships for students studying agriculture at a Scottish University, Scottish Agricultural College or other further education institution in Scotland; Providing funding for applied research in an aspect of agriculture whether by a research institute, university or other educational institution in Scotland.
- **funding_amounts**: The website does not specify grant amounts. The Trust's annual expenditure for the year ending 31 Mar 2022 was £185,016.
- **deadlines**: The closing date for applications is the 15th of November. The Trustees meet up once a year to decide upon grants.

### Innovation Fund
- **description**: In 2016, the Trustees resolved to set up an Innovation Fund, to encourage innovative on-farm practical research of benefit to Scottish agriculture. The project can be based on livestock, crops, buildings, machinery or farm diversification. It is hoped that applications will come from farmers and farming advisors, rather than from the academic community. Each year, the sum of £10,000 is earmarked for awards to applicants to this Fund. The awards are intended as financial support for the costs associated with the implementation of new ideas.
- **funding_amounts**: Each year, the sum of £10,000 is earmarked for awards to applicants to this Fund.
- **deadlines**: The closing date for applications is the 15th of November. The Trustees meet up once a year to decide upon awards from the Innovation Fund.
