### Soko Scholarship
- **description**: Scholarships for women from disadvantaged backgrounds in Malawi to study vocational degrees at one of Soko's partner universities. Each scholarship covers the full cost of tuition and includes a small allowance for accommodation and/or study materials.
- **funding_amounts**: Each Soko Scholarship covers the full cost of tuition, and a small allowance, which students can use for accommodation and/or study materials. The allowance is currently MK100,000 per academic year. This allowance is paid in one lump sum near the beginning of the academic year.
